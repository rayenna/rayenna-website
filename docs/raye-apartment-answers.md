## Q: We are a 60-flat society. What subsidy can we get?

For Group Housing Society / RWA **common facilities** (including EV charging), PM Surya Ghar CFA is **₹18,000 per kW**, up to **500 kW** capacity at **3 kW per house**, with that ceiling **inclusive of** individual rooftop plants residents already installed. Source: pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

Illustration only: a 60-flat society has an eligible-capacity ceiling of **180 kW** (3 × 60). A **30 kW** common-area plant would attract up to **₹5.4 lakh** (30 × 18,000). Final eligibility is confirmed on the national portal.

Individual flat plants (where the society permits dedicated roof/terrace access) still use the residential slabs: ₹30,000/kW up to 2 kW; ₹18,000/kW for the third kW; capped at ₹78,000 above 3 kW.

## Q: What are the three ways our building can go solar?

1. **Individual flat/terrace plant** — dedicated roof access + society permission; residential PM Surya Ghar rules.  
2. **Common-area plant** — one plant for lifts, pumps, corridor/basement lighting, EV charging; GHS/RWA CFA. Under KSERC 2025, net metering for common-area service connections of multi-storied residential buildings / colonies is **1–500 kW** (hybrid storage rules apply above 25 kW).  
3. **Shared (virtual) net metering** — one plant, credits split across flat meters. For multi-storied residential / residents’ associations: energy storage of at least **20%** of generation potential; **minimum 10 kW**; Lead person; society resolution; credit-sharing agreement; KSEB approval. Common-amenity meters may join. Ground implementation is still maturing.

## Q: Will solar run the lifts at night?

Panels generate in daylight. Night loads use the grid unless you add storage. Daytime common loads often align with generation. We do not claim typical percentage savings.
